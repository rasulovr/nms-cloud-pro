Privacy Policy

RMS Pro Backup · Effective 26 August 2026

RMS Pro Backup is a private technical service used to create encrypted backups of the RMS Pro restaurant-management system.

Google Drive access

The service requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. It can create, read, update, and remove only the backup files it creates. It does not access unrelated files in the Google account.

Backup data and security

Backup archives may contain RMS database and storage data. They are encrypted with AES-256 before upload to Google Drive. Encryption keys are stored separately from Google Drive and are not included in the backup files.

Sharing and retention

Backup data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Encrypted files are retained for up to 90 days and then automatically removed by the backup service.
