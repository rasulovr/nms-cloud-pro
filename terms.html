RMS Pro Backup · Effective 26 August 2026

RMS Pro Backup is a private operational service for the RMS Pro system. It is not a public consumer application.

Purpose

The service creates encrypted backups of RMS Pro data, related storage files, and the corresponding application source revision, then uploads them to the designated Google Drive account.

Access

Google Drive access is restricted to files created by this service through the drive.file permission. The service must not be used to access unrelated Google Drive content.

Availability and recovery

Backups are an additional recovery measure. Restoration must be performed deliberately by an authorised RMS Pro administrator and verified before production use.
